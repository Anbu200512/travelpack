Coverage

What the warranty covers

Cover runs from the date on your receipt: 3 years on value ranges, 5 years on core ranges and 7 years on premium polycarbonate shells.

Covered

  • Wheel, axle and spinner failures in normal use
  • Telescopic handle stems that jam, slip or won't lock
  • Zip teeth splitting and slider separation
  • TSA lock and number-lock mechanism defects
  • Shell cracks and handle mounts on the frame
  • Stitching and lining failure on soft-side cases and bags

Not covered

  • Airline and baggage-handler damage (crush, impact, scuffs)
  • Over-packing, dropped or dragged cases
  • Cosmetic wear: scratches, faded shell, worn corners
  • Lost keys, forgotten combinations, cut locks
  • Bags modified, repaired elsewhere or used for hire
  • Second-hand purchases without an original receipt

Keep your receipt. A digital photo or card statement is enough — bring it to the counter or attach it to the enquiry form below. Without proof of purchase we can still repair the bag, but as chargeable out-of-warranty work.

Process

How a claim runs, start to finish

  1. 1

    Tell us what happened

  3. 3

  4. 4

    Typical turnaround is 3–5 working days; parts orders may take longer.

Repair bench

What we fix, for any brand

Wheel & axle replacement

Spinner and inline wheels, any diameter in stock.

Handle systems

Telescopic tubes, buttons, top and side carry handles.

Zips & sliders

Replacements, re-stitching and slider re-joins.

Locks & TSA keys

Number locks re-cut, TSA cores replaced.

Shell & frame repair

Crack patches, hinge pins, corner guards.

Bags & backpacks

Straps, buckles, seams and lining re-stitched.
