Our story

We started because travellers kept coming back with broken bags

In 2009 the shop was a corner unit selling a handful of cases and a rack of duffles. The part that paid the rent was the queue of people arriving with a wheel in one hand and a receipt in the other — bags they had bought somewhere else, with nobody willing to fix them.

So we learned to repair. Then we started choosing stock by how well it survived our own bench: which wheel assemblies last, which zip gauges do not jam, which handles stay tight after a hundred departures. Everything on the floor today earned its place that way.

We do not run flash sales or badge up prices to cut them. We will happily spend twenty minutes showing you the difference between two cases, and just as happily tell you that the smaller one is the right one for your trip.

Behind the shop floor

The bench is why the warranty means something

Claims do not get boxed up and shipped away. They get opened on our own bench, diagnosed by our own technician and either fixed free under warranty or quoted in writing before anything chargeable starts.

  • Wheels, axles, handles, zips, locks and shells handled in-house
  • Spare parts kept for every range we have ever stocked
  • Any brand accepted — bought from us or not
  • Typical turnaround of three to five working days
